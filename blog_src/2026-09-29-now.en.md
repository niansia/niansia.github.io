## Shipped this month

- **Taiwan Exam is public**: an Agent Skill that has an AI write original GSAT practice exams to the current curriculum. It launched on September 28, together with a short film.
- **LumiGrid runs in the browser**: both networks of the low-light enhancer run on the visitor's own device, so photos are never uploaded.
- **Adversarial Lab is live**: FGSM / PGD attacks, decision maps and an adversarially trained model, all running in the browser, now also its own open-source repository.
- **A gallery for Taiwan Exam exams**: people share the exams they generate, so students without a paid AI plan can download them and practise. The first 21 are up; every upload is scanned for malware, stripped of hidden content and checked for personal data.
- **The site moved to niansia.com**: the old address forwards there. HTTPS, DNSSEC and a block on AI-training crawlers came with the move, along with a blog and a guestbook.
- **LINE communities for GSAT students**: the two groups I run have over five thousand members between them; they are now on the about page and in the gallery.

## Next

- **CVPR 2027**: a vision-language model (VLM) topic, due November 16 (AoE).
- **ICCV 2027**: a Diffusion Transformer (DiT) topic, in preparation.
- **COLM 2027**: planned; the topic is still being scoped.
