Research Meeting Coach

Research workflowEarly alpha

An evidence-grounded Agent Skill that turns raw research progress into a decision-ready advisor meeting. It separates observations from interpretation, carries unresolved requests forward, and frames one advisor-answerable decision without inventing completion.

Open the interactive portfolio →Source ↗Evidence audit ↗

v0.3.5-alpha — When you use the Skill, a model writes the meeting brief. What this page runs is the part that does not depend on a model: the Skill’s own validators, re-run for this page on the repository’s synthetic example. The example outputs were written during development and are labelled that way.

How a week becomes one decision

  1. Typed facts — Every result and claim becomes a fact with a quoted source line, a unit, a condition and whether the value was exact.
  2. Prior actions — What the advisor asked for last time, with its real status. Unfinished stays unfinished.
  3. Evidence boundary — Observation, interpretation, hypothesis and proposal stay separate; gaps are ranked Critical to Low, with no fake probabilities.
  4. One decision — The meeting ends with one question the advisor can answer, with options that were proposed or supplied, never invented.
  5. Deterministic gates — The state file, its sources, every number in the brief and any advisor profile are checked by scripts, not by the model.

Who does what — The model reads your notes and writes the brief. Scripts check what can be checked: that each quote is on the cited line, that every number in the brief matches a typed fact with the same condition and qualifier, and that any advisor preference comes from recorded behaviour. A persona from someone’s nationality, institution or prestige is refused.

  • Observation ≠ interpretation — “Accuracy dropped” and “compression caused it” are different claims.
  • Themes ≠ predictions — Likely questions are preparation aids, never probabilities.
  • Behaviour, not background — Personalisation needs recorded feedback or a pattern across three meetings.
  • Unfinished stays unfinished — A half-done control is reported as half done.
