title: The site moves to niansia.com
date: 2026-09-29
tags: [Website]
---

The site now lives on its own domain, [niansia.com](https://niansia.com/). The old niansia.github.io address forwards there, so existing links keep working.

The move was a good moment to cover the basics: HTTPS everywhere, DNSSEC, a block on crawlers that collect AI training data, and a [security.txt](/.well-known/security.txt) that says how to report a security problem.

I also opened a [blog](/#blog) and a [guestbook](/#guestbook): a monthly update and paper notes on the blog, and guestbook notes appear once I have approved them.
