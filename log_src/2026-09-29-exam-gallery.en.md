A free AI account often runs out of quota before it finishes a whole exam, so I opened a [shared gallery](/exams/zh-tw/): everyone shares one exam made with Taiwan Exam, and everyone else can download it and practise. The first 21 are my own, covering Chinese, Chinese writing, English, Math A, Math B, social studies and science.

![The exam gallery: search and subject/AI filters on top; every exam can be previewed or downloaded, questions and solutions alike](img/exam-gallery.png)

Uploaded files are scanned for malware, stripped of links, forms and hidden content, and checked for personal data before they go public. They live in a Hugging Face dataset under CC BY-NC 4.0.

The same day, the gallery started listing the two LINE communities for GSAT students that I run, with over five thousand members between them.
